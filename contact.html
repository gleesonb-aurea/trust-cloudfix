Vulnerability Reporting

We take security vulnerabilities seriously and appreciate responsible disclosure. If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in CloudFix:

  1. Submit your findings using the contact form above, selecting \"Vulnerability Report\" as the subject, with full details of the vulnerability.
  2. Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability before we've had a chance to remediate it.
  3. We will acknowledge receipt within 24 hours and provide an initial assessment within 72 hours.
  4. We will keep you updated on remediation progress and notify you when the fix is deployed.
  5. We ask that you allow 90 days for remediation before any public disclosure.
